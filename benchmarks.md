The following benchmark suites have been used to evaluate Z3str4 and other string solvers.

[Kaluza](https://github.com/z3str/Z3-str/tree/master/benchmark/1.kaluza) (legacy Z3-str archive)

[IBM PISA](https://github.com/z3str/Z3-str/tree/master/benchmark/2.pisa/cvc4) (12 SMT-LIB instances, CVC4 version)

[IBM AppScan](https://github.com/z3str/Z3-str/tree/master/benchmark/3.appscan/cvc4) (8 SMT-LIB instances, CVC4 version)

[PyEx](https://explore.smt-lib.org/family/176.html) (in the SMT-LIB Reynolds collection; see the `pyex/` entries)

The SMT-LIB versions of Kaluza and PyEx are also available in the `QF_SLIA` archive from the [official SMT-LIB benchmark release](https://zenodo.org/records/16740866).

[Stringfuzz suites](http://stringfuzz.dmitryblotsky.com/problems/) (external link)
